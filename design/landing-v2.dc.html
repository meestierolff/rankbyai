FAQ

Straight answers about AI visibility tracking.

What’s the catch with “pay once”?⌄

There’s no subscription to cancel. You buy the software once and keep using it. The data still costs money, but you pay DataForSEO directly, at their price.

Why does the price keep going up?⌄

Early buyers take the most risk, so they get the lowest price. Every {{ tier }} sales the price rises ${{ priceStep }}. The counter sets it, not a timer.

Do I need a DataForSEO account?⌄

For scans at cost, yes. It’s pay-as-you-go, so you only pay for what you use. If you’d rather not, buy included scans in packs.

What does a scan really cost?⌄

A 7×7 grid is 49 points. Each point asks each AI engine once and checks Maps once: roughly {{ scanLabel }} for all four engines at current rates. The app shows the estimate before you run it.

Where do the AI answers come from?⌄

Through your DataForSEO key, which asks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini the same “best {service} in {area}” prompt at every grid point. Maps rank comes from DataForSEO too.

Is RankbyAI the only tool that does this?⌄

No. Local Falcon and BrightLocal both offer AI visibility tracking. We differ on price model: pay once, API calls at cost, bulk prospect reports and an open API and MCP server.

Can my clients see the results?⌄

Yes. Share a live report link or send a PDF, under your own brand.

Can I use it with Claude or ChatGPT?⌄

Yes. Connect the MCP server or call the REST API, and your agent can run scans, audits and reports.
